# Preliminary Shackleton correspondence inspection

Two tentative physical feature correspondences were selected by the agent in the browser on 11 November 2026. They are distinct from the arbitrary developer controls used in build 0.9. Neither correspondence has independent review or accepted registration status.

The first selection used the apparent center of a large northern rim crater as a fit landmark. The photo's shadowed interior and the smoother terrain representation make its center uncertain. A second, degraded mid-rim bowl was selected as a check landmark and excluded from that fit. The views were inspected at 2×, using northeast and northwest synthetic hillshades as shape aids.

The 25.4 m check mismatch is a result of these particular tentative selections, not an estimated absolute geolocation error or validated registration bound. Manual center-selection uncertainty has not been quantified. A zero residual at the single fit landmark is an algebraic consequence of fitting that point, not an accuracy check. These observations do not justify shifting or draping the image. They also cannot test the source's absolute-accuracy estimate: relative alignment, manual feature ambiguity and absolute accuracy are different quantities.

The current workbench can restore an exported assessment only when image, metadata, DEM and inspection-asset identities match. It recomputes every result and ignores any stored approval claim. Imports are bounded to 2 MB and 200 pairs. A rejected import preserves current pairs. Older software versions may be restored if the exact area-specific source assets still match; changes in another area's catalog do not invalidate otherwise identical local evidence.

The appropriate next scientific step is additional clearly identifiable, spatially distributed landmarks, separate check points and correspondence review. Difficult or shadow-obscured matches should be rejected. Three fit and three check points spanning 20% of both crop axes form an exploratory review threshold only; meeting it does not automatically establish registration.
