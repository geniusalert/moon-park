Uncertainty & rankings ↗ · Scientific validation brief ↗ · Frame cross-check ↗ · Time sampling ↗ · Regional impact ↗

Constraints are checked separately. Combined availability is total overlap, not a promise of a continuous activity window. Earth-center visibility does not guarantee a radio link. Sun visibility is not electrical output. Published-region interior samples use separately calculated nominal terrain and coordinate-specific SPICE observations. Their results do not inherit the benchmark points’ clone support. Uniform ±0.25° horizon perturbations are a sensitivity experiment, not a statistical confidence interval.

Regional LOLA terrain and frame-matched SPICE geometry are connected. Horizons comparisons are recorded; finite coverage and terrain resolution still limit accuracy. Eclipse and thermal models are absent. See the research plan for data sources, validation gates, and delivery status.
