Visibility requirements · optional limits
What do these limits mean?

These are scenario settings you choose for comparison. The starting values are examples, not official mission requirements.

Maximum continuous darkness
Longest allowed interval without modeled Sun visibility. This does not predict battery capacity or thermal survival.
Minimum total Sun and Earth overlap
Total time both are visible; it can be split across separate intervals. Direct Earth visibility is not a radio-link guarantee.
Continuous activity needed
One uninterrupted interval with both Sun and Earth visible. Scattered overlap hours do not satisfy it.

A passing result means only that this geometric model meets these selected limits. It does not decide whether a site is safe to land at.

Surface, sky and animation limits

The selected result supplies the Moon's measured terrain, Sun/Earth directions and visibility at the selected UTC and observer height. The local surface uses NASA LOLA elevations at true scale, including lunar curvature. Where only a reference sphere is supported, the surface stays smooth and explicitly omits terrain. Display detail does not establish landing hazard clearance.

Sun and Earth positions update with the mission conditions slider and the main replay. The guides originate at the fixed planning observer, not the moving lander. Symbols are enlarged and their radial distances are illustrative; the compass always shows their calculated azimuths. Hidden directions remain visible as dashed guides. There is no atmospheric refraction. Studio fill can be enabled for legibility and is labeled.

Your Apollo model is normalized to 7 m. Approach, braking, plume and dust follow an illustrative 66-second animation over the surface, independent of the sampled mission clock. This is not a solved flight trajectory: fuel, thrust, guidance, spacecraft-altitude visibility, collisions, landing safety and dust physics are not predicted. Surface display height is centered at the sampled landing point; its interpolation offset is recorded in the surface information.

The supplied whole-Moon model remains available as a compressed-scale demonstration and overview. Its texture is not registered to the calculated location. The measured surface uses neutral shading, not a registered photograph.
